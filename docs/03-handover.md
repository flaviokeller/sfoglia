### Photos — please read this one

**Resize photos to under 500 KB before uploading.** A photo straight from a
phone is often 5–8 MB. Uploading several of those will make your site slow to
load and slow to update, and it cannot be fully undone later.

On a Mac: open in Preview → Tools → Adjust Size → set the width to 2000 pixels →
save. On Windows: open in Photos → Resize → Medium.

Also fill in the **Bildbeschreibung (Alt-Text)** field. It describes the photo
for people using screen readers and helps Google understand the page. One short
sentence is enough — "Unser Behandlungszimmer mit Blick auf den See", not "Bild".
